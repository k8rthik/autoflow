# Harbor — Pre-seed note

Harbor is a marketplace for chartering small commercial fishing boats for scientific sampling. Research institutions currently spend $600M a year on vessel time and wait an average of five months for a berth on a research ship, while thousands of licensed fishing boats sit idle in the off-season. Harbor has completed 40 charters for six universities in its first four months, with a 20% take rate on an average charter of $8,000, and every institution that has chartered once has chartered again.

The founder spent eight years as a marine field technician coordinating vessel logistics for two oceanographic institutes. We suggest a $300K check into a $1.5M pre-seed. The main risk is that regulatory permitting for scientific work on commercial vessels varies by state and could slow expansion.
