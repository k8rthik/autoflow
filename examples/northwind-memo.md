# Northwind Diagnostics — Series A

We propose investing $5M in Northwind's $18M Series A.

## The problem and the market

Community hospitals run about 4,000 pathology labs in the US, and a shortage of pathologists means biopsy turnaround has stretched to an average of nine days at these sites, against two days at academic centers. That gap matters clinically and it matters financially: hospitals lose patients to referral centers when results are slow. Digital pathology has been technically feasible for a decade, but adoption at community hospitals is below 5% because existing systems were designed and priced for large academic labs. We estimate the community segment as a $1.8B annual opportunity for digitization and remote read services. One caveat: our market estimate assumes reimbursement for remote reads holds at current levels, which is not guaranteed.

## What Northwind does

Northwind installs a low-cost slide scanner and connects the hospital to a network of remote pathologists who read overnight. Turnaround at live sites has fallen from nine days to under 48 hours. The scanner is third-party hardware; Northwind's value is the software workflow and the pathologist network, and the company keeps 55% gross margin on the read fee. We note that the pathologist network is a supply-constrained asset and scaling it may prove harder than the software.

## Traction

Northwind is live in 14 hospitals across four states, up from four hospitals a year ago. Revenue is $2.2M annualized. Each hospital signs a three-year contract, and no hospital has churned. The pipeline includes two health systems that would each add more than ten sites. Our concern on traction is that the sales cycle to health systems is long and the two pipeline deals have both slipped a quarter.

## Team

The CEO is a former hospital COO who ran two community hospitals. The chief medical officer is a board-certified pathologist who built the remote-read program at a regional health system. The head of engineering previously led the imaging platform at a radiology AI company.

## Recommendation

We recommend the investment. The problem is acute, the product is live and working, and the team combines hospital operations with pathology.
