# Quill — Seed Memo

**Ask:** $2.5M seed at $15M post. We would take $1M.

## Summary

Quill lets renters report their rent payments to the credit bureaus and build a score without taking on debt. Young renters have no credit file, rent reporting demonstrably fixes that, and Quill is growing fast without spending on acquisition. The founders have built exactly this product before. We recommend investing.

## Why now

There are 22 million Gen Z renters in the US, and roughly 70% of them have no credit history that would qualify them for a prime card. They pay rent every month, but that payment is invisible to the bureaus. All three bureaus began accepting rent tradelines from third-party reporters in 2024, which is what makes a consumer product possible now.

## Product

The user connects a bank account, Quill identifies the rent payment, and reports it monthly. For a thin-file user, reporting rent raises the score by an average of 40 points within six months, based on Quill's own cohort data across 3,000 users. The premium tier adds a rent-backed savings feature and costs $4 a month; 30% of active users buy it.

## Traction

Quill has 18,000 monthly active users, growing 25% month over month, with zero paid acquisition to date. Growth comes from referrals inside apartment buildings and a partnership with two property managers. Of users who connect a bank account, 60% are still active after 90 days.

## Team

The two founders built the rent-payment product at a property-management software company that processed $2B a year in rent, and left after that company was acquired. They know the payment rails and the bureau reporting process from the inside.

## Risks

The bureaus could change their reporting rules. Large banks could bundle rent reporting into a checking account for free. The premium attach rate may fall as the user base broadens beyond early adopters.

## Recommendation

Invest $1M. The combination of a large underserved population, a product that measurably works, and organic growth makes this a strong seed bet.
